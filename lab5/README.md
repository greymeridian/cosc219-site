# COSC219 Lab 5

## Summary
This lab uses JavaScript array methods to analyze a dataset of ten Okanagan trails, including their distances, difficulty ratings, and closure statuses.

The final report shows that 8 of the 10 trails are open, with a combined open distance of 54.6 km, and Mission Creek is the longest open trail.

## Challenges

The most challenging part was recalling concepts from the lectures and applying them to write the correct conditions for each iterator method. VS Code's autocomplete helped identify mistakes and suggest corrections, making the implementation process easier.
